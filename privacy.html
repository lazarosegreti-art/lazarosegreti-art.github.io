Privacy Policy

ASMR Shorts Automation is a personal application used by its owner to publish original AI-generated videos on YouTube.

Google Data Access

The application accesses the authorized YouTube channel's information and uploads videos on behalf of its owner.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials are stored securely in GitHub Actions Secrets. Operational publication records may be stored in a private repository.

Third-Party Services

GitHub runs the automation, fal.ai generates the videos, and YouTube receives the videos for publication.

Data Deletion

The owner can revoke Google access through Google Account permissions and delete the stored credentials.
